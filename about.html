About Us

Our History

Pick n Pay was founded in 1967 by Raymond Ackerman in Cape Town, growing from a single store into one of South Africa's leading retail chains. Our branch continues that legacy, serving the local community with quality groceries and everyday essentials.

Our Mission

To make quality food and household products accessible and affordable, while building lasting relationships with the communities we serve.

Our Vision

To be the first choice for local shoppers by offering value, freshness, and excellent service every day.

Our Team

Our branch is run by a dedicated team of local staff who know our customers by name and take pride in keeping our shelves stocked and our service friendly.
